Questions

Straight answers.

Is this the same as the Blueprint?

No. The Blueprint is the full self-paced curriculum — the system, on your own time. The Cohort is the live room: the same seven phases, walked together over eight weeks, with me in the room and a brotherhood around you. Most men do the Blueprint first; it's the strongest foundation to walk in with.

What does it cost?

The investment is $1,997 for the eight-week cohort. It's still application-based — this isn't a checkout-button decision, and I read every application myself before we ever talk about a seat.

Do I have to be on camera?

Yes. The room works because men show up as themselves, face to face. That's the point — and it's part of what makes it different from anything you can hide behind.

I'm still in the worst of it. Is it too early?

Not necessarily. Some men come in still on the floor; some come up for air first. Tell me where you are in the application and we'll figure out the right timing together.

What if it's not a fit?

Then I'll tell you, and I'll point you toward what is — the Blueprint, the Challenge, or simply the free guide. No pressure, no hard sell. The wrong room helps no one.
